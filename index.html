How PicSwiper works

  • Swipe right (or tap Keep) to keep an item.
  • Swipe left (or tap Delete) to mark it for deletion.
  • Undo reverses your last decision.
  • Nothing is deleted until you open Review, check the items you marked and confirm. iOS then asks you once more.

Frequently asked questions

Where do deleted items go?
To the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for up to 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, they're also removed from your other devices.

Why doesn't my free storage go up right away?
iOS frees the space once items are removed from Recently Deleted. The sizes PicSwiper shows are estimates of how much space items take in your library.

PicSwiper only shows some of my photos.
You may have given it access to selected items only. Go to Settings → PicSwiper → Photos and choose Full Access, or use Select Items on the Home screen.

Will I lose my progress if I close the app?
No. Your sessions are saved on your iPhone after every swipe and reopen where you left off.

Does PicSwiper upload my photos?
Never. Everything happens on your iPhone. There's no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
